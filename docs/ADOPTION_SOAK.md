# KevinOS 30-day local adoption soak

Continue this scorecard when v0.51 becomes Kevin's daily driver. It is local evidence for pruning and promotion—not external analytics and not a reason to add more rooms.

## Daily check (days 1–30)

Attention evidence is optional. If enabled, inspect the local current/prior seven-day summary only when useful; it is not a required daily ritual. Record whether the single signal was useful, noisy, or absent. Disable or clear it at any time without changing NOW.

## Weekly review (days 7, 14, 21, 30)

- Opened KevinOS at least 5 of 7 days.
- Capture was visible within 10 seconds.
- First focus was set or confirmed within two minutes.
- Close ritual completed at least three times.
- AI proposal backlog remained at three or fewer.
- Accepted, edited, rejected, escalated, and failed proposal patterns are visible in the local Proposal Inbox summary.
- Zero unresolved data-loss, import, snapshot, recovery, or convergence failures.
- Run the manual read-only backup drill at least once during the soak; record only whether it passed and whether the result was understandable.
- Inspect Recent important changes after one AI apply/Undo and one import or restore; keep the pilot only if the receipts make recovery decisions clearer without feeling like surveillance.
- If the Calm Friction pilot is explicitly enabled, inspect its seven-day NOW/Capture aggregate. Keep it only if the fixed categories reveal a change worth making without feeling like surveillance; Clear local marks at any time. Do not infer meaning from a single mark.
- Inspect local `roomStats`: demote rooms that add noise and preserve the rooms that earn repeat use.

## Day-30 decision

Record one of: `KEEP`, `DEMOTE`, `REMOVE`, or `RUN A SMALLER TEST` for each recurring friction point. Decide separately whether to keep or remove the Calm Friction pilot. Only consider one-at-a-time Capture triage if the soak shows repeated Inbox friction that the current quick capture cannot resolve. Prefer removing or simplifying before adding another room, card, provider, or automation. Broad public/SaaS productization remains Track B and requires a separate decision after this personal-use soak.
