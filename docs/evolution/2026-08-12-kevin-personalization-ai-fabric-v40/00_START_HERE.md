## Two finish lines

### Preactivation

Complete every K-1 through K9 task, prove every credentialless contract with mocks/synthetic fixtures, run the full system without keys or network, pass the secret scan, and pass `sh tools/run-evolution-gates.sh preactivation`.

### Final activation

Only then begin K10. Ask Kevin once for accounts/keys, explicitly tell him not to paste secrets into chat, use the silent local ceremony, run at most one bounded synthetic probe per configured provider, retain only redacted facts, and never deploy or mutate remote secrets without separate approval.
